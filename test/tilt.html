Seated head tilt: rotate the master head, mouths and blinks included

Drag the slider to lean the head about the neck, and see how far it can go before it looks wrong. The mouths and blinks cycle on their own so you can check they follow the tilt. Positive leans clockwise (towards the right of the image).

Left: tilted head. Right: the same frame with no tilt, to compare against. Only the head's own pixels (inside the blue outline) are rotated; the neck, collar and jacket stay put, and the yellow cross is the pivot, just under the chin. Plain mode just rotates the pixels, RotSprite-style scales up 8x first and picks the most common colour in each cell on the way back.
