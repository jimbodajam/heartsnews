Run each as a fresh request, attaching `standing-master.jpg` (the full-length standing Isla, palm raised towards the screen on the viewer's right). Never attach a previous variant.
Ask for PNG if possible. Keep the output the same size and framing as the master (848 x 1264, figure in the same place).

Every prompt below is complete: copy the whole block and paste it as is.

These are "change only the arms" edits. The head, face, hair, torso, skirt, legs and shoes must stay pixel-identical, because the face frames (mouths, blinks, glances) are laid over the head at a fixed position, and swapping between poses should never make her feet slide. Left and right below always mean the viewer's left and right (screen left and screen right).

Order: 1 and 2 first (a relaxed pose to rest in, and a pointing pose, which together with the master are enough to stop her looking stiff), then 3 and 4, then the rest.

Check each result before keeping it: the head should sit in exactly the same place as in the master, the feet and skirt should be unchanged, and the pixel size should match. If the head moved or the feet shifted, add "the head must stay at exactly the same pixel position as in the original" to the end of the prompt and run it again.
