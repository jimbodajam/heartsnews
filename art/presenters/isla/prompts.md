# Isla sprite prompts

Run each as a fresh request, attaching the master image (never a previous variant). Ask for PNG if possible.
`master-original.jpg` is the starting master. Run Step 0 first and use its result as the master for everything else.

## Step 0: fix the master

Edit this image. Remove the two small white dashes under the eyes, replacing them with plain skin matching the surrounding colours. Keep everything else exactly identical: same pixels, same position, same size, same colours, same outline, same flat magenta background. Do not move or resize the head.

## Lead-in for mouths and brows

Edit this image. Keep everything exactly identical: same pixels, same position, same size, same colours, same outline, same flat magenta background. Do not move or resize the head. Keep the eyebrows and eyes exactly as in the original: same shape, same position, relaxed and neutral. Change only this one thing:

1. the mouth is closed with a gentle slight smile.
2. the lips are slightly parted, relaxed, a thin dark gap and no visible teeth.
3. the mouth is a wide relaxed "ee" shape, with a thin strip of the top teeth visible.
4. the mouth is a small round "oh", lips rounded and pushed forward, dark inside, no teeth.
5. the mouth is open about half as wide as in a shout, relaxed and mid-speech as if saying "ah" in a calm conversation, showing the top teeth and a little tongue.
6. the lips are pressed together, closed and neutral, as if about to say "m" or "b".

## Lead-in for eyes and brows

Edit this image. Keep everything exactly identical: same pixels, same position, same size, same colours, same outline, same flat magenta background. Do not move or resize the head. Keep the mouth, nose, hair and brows exactly as in the original. Change only this one thing:

7. both eyes are fully closed, as in a blink, with the upper lashes drawn as a curved dark line.
8. both eyes are open as before, but the irises look to the viewer's left (screen left), pupils shifted about two pixels.
9. both eyes are open as before, but the irises look to the viewer's right (screen right), pupils shifted about two pixels.
10. the eyebrows are raised slightly higher, arched, in a lightly surprised or interested look. The eyes stay the same.

(For 10, use the eyes lead-in but drop "and brows" from the keep-list.)

## Hand gesture (optional)

Edit this image. Keep the head, hair, face, neck, jacket, blouse and background exactly identical: same pixels, same position, same size, same colours and outline. Change only the presenter's right arm (on the viewer's left): bend it at the elbow so the forearm points upward with the open palm facing the viewer at chest height, fingers together, in a natural explaining gesture. Do not change the other arm.

## Order

Step 0, then 5 and 1, then 7. That is the minimum to see lip-sync and blinking working.
